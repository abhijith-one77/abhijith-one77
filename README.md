### 🔬 About Me

I am a physician-scientist working at the intersection of clinical medicine and computational biology. With a foundation in medicine, I design and implement bioinformatics pipelines and machine learning systems to solve complex diagnostic challenges.

My work focuses on translating multi-omic data into clinically actionable insights, particularly in oncology, neurodegeneration, and longevity science—driving the shift toward precision medicine.

**Key problem areas I work on:**
- Resolving diagnostic ambiguity in thyroid FNAC using long-read sequencing  
- Building multi-class diagnostic models using deep learning  
- Modelling biological ageing (PhenoAge) and neurological outcomes  
- Identifying and validating longevity biomarkers through multi-omics
